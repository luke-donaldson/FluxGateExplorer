Assumptions: the bed does not change, so a change in surface elevation is a change in thickness. Velocity is treated as depth-averaged after the scaling factor is applied. Values change in annual steps and cumulative discharge uses the trapezoid rule. Gt of ice and Gt of water equivalent are the same quantity, and 361.8 Gt is taken as 1 mm of global mean sea level.
